# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Subtil Controls for YouTube does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

## What the extension does with page data

On YouTube watch pages, the extension reads the page URL, the video identifier, the player configuration, and the page language. It does so locally in your browser, only to provide its audio-only and loop controls. This information is not retained and is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

- No analytics, trackers, or advertising.
- No account and no developer-operated server.
- No browser storage, cookies, or viewing history.
- No remotely hosted code.

Audio streams and thumbnails are requested directly from YouTube as part of your normal playback session, under YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

## Permissions

The extension runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*` and requests no other Chrome permission.

## Changes

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
